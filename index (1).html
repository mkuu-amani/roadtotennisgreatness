Walsall Aces · the charter

The Road To Tennis Greatness

One player. Every age group. From the club court to the oldest band the sport still crowns. The work is filmed, counted and written down.

The vision

To become the greatest tennis player the age groups have ever seen. Win the 60s. Then the 65s. Then every band after that, up to the oldest championship still played.

This week

Three court sessions, two strength sessions, one real set. The week resets each Monday.

The ladder

Each band is a title to be taken, not a hobby to be sampled. Mark the one you are in.

Log a session

One fault for a fortnight. Count first serves in.

Match book

Score, and the one pattern that beat you. That pattern is next month’s drill.
